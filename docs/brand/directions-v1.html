Five ways Extrudo could look

Each direction has a logo, a colour palette, a type pairing, a sample of the tool icons, and a preview of the app itself in that style. You can switch any app preview between light and dark, and click a colour to copy its hex. Pick one, or mix parts, e.g. "A's logo with E's colours".

How to choose

Tell me the letter in the chat, plus anything you'd change. I'll then refine that direction into the final brand: exact logo geometry, a one-colour version for stickers and laser engraving, a favicon, design tokens for the app's light and dark themes, and a brief for the full tool icon set (about 60 icons). All of it goes into docs/05-brand.md.
