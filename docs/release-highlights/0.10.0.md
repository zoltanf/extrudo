- **Inch designs**: a design has its own unit (mm, cm, m, in) and precision in the browser's Document settings. Inch designs get a fractional grid and snapping, fractions and feet-inches in expressions (`3/8 in`, `1 1/2"`, `5' 6"`), optional fraction display, and tools that open with inch sizes.
- **Annotations**: the Annotate tool (`N`) pins numbered notes to faces, edges, points and sketch geometry, in the 3D view and in sketches. Notes are saved in the design, listed in the browser and can be ticked off. Agents read and write them over MCP, so "make the hole at #3 M4" is a complete instruction.
- **Threads for printing**: a 3D printing (45°) thread profile with print sizes and a Fit choice. A thread brings its face to size (a Ø5 hole becomes M5's minor), fills a hole past its root, and runs its lead-in through a chamfered end.
- **Sketching**: dimensions from a circle's or arc's edge, Project takes an earlier sketch's curves, Auto-project shows what you can snap to, snapping to a face's centre, Midpoint on a line or axis, a new parameter straight from a dimension's editor, and a tidier sketch palette. Undo after Finish Sketch reopens the sketch at its last edit.
- **3D mouse**: a 3Dconnexion SpaceMouse or SpaceNavigator moves the view in Chrome, Edge and the desktop app, with or without the vendor's driver.
- **Mesh to Solid**: rounded rims, tori, cones and thin fillets become real curved faces far more often, with fewer facets, and converted bodies survive a STEP round trip.
- **Other CAD files (desktop)**: the desktop app imports .sldprt, .ipt, .iam, .catpart, .fcstd, .sat, .igs and more by converting them to STEP with the bundled cadmpeg 0.6.8.
- **Also**: Emboss on several faces and Tangent to the face, named views keep their section, Import Model centres new imports, Report a Bug can include the recent actions, and a much fuller tool guide with clips for every sketch tool.
