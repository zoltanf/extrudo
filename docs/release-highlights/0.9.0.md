- **Mesh to Solid**: an imported STL, 3MF or OBJ mesh becomes a solid with real faces (Solid › Modify, or Convert to Solid… on the body), so fillets, Offset Face and other features work on it. Zero-volume flaps a slicer's boolean left behind are dropped.
- **Remove Faces**: take faces out of a body and let the faces around them close the gap, so a hole or a fillet goes away, even on an imported body.
- **Offset Face on imported bodies**: flat faces that wouldn't offset before now move, so converted meshes can be pushed and pulled.
- **Render Picture**: the new Make tab (formerly 3D Print) renders a presentable picture of the design, in the browser with the app's own renderer, or in the desktop app with an installed Blender (4.2 or newer). Includes a Workbench scene with a 10 mm cutting mat.
- **Faster and more responsive**: the kernel starts while the home screen shows, so a design opened from it no longer waits for the kernel (about 0.8 s to ready instead of 2 s on a first visit). Editing and hovering are snappier, long kernel calls show their progress, and a long model update can be cancelled.
- **.f3d import**: face deletions become Remove Faces steps.
