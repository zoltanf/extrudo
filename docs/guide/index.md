# Extrudo docs

Extrudo is parametric CAD for 3D printing that runs in your browser: you sketch
a shape, give it dimensions, turn it into a solid, and the timeline remembers
every step so you can change a number and watch the part follow. Open the app at
[{{APP_URL}}]({{APP_URL}}/).

These pages are the written guide:

- [The app in five minutes](./getting-started.md): the screen, moving the view, finding commands
  and where your designs are kept.
- [Sketching and constraints](./sketching.md): planes, drawing, constraints, dimensions and
  profiles.
- [Features and the timeline](./features-and-timeline.md): dialogs, editing, rolling back,
  reordering and lost references.
- [Parameters and expressions](./parameters.md): named values, units, the Customizer and print
  tolerance.

There is also a [page for every tool](./tools/index.md). Tutorials and a gallery of examples are
on their way; they appear in the sidebar as they land.
If you want to build designs from code, the [API reference](../api/README.md)
covers the document API, scripts, macros and plugins.
