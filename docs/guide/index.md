# Extrudo docs

Extrudo is parametric CAD for 3D printing that runs in your browser: you sketch
a shape, give it dimensions, turn it into a solid, and the timeline remembers
every step so you can change a number and watch the part follow. Open the app at
[{{APP_URL}}]({{APP_URL}}/).

These pages are the written guide. Tutorials, a page for every tool and a
gallery of examples are on their way; they appear in the sidebar as they land.
If you want to build designs from code, the [API reference](../api/README.md)
covers the document API, scripts, macros and plugins.
