A · A mode, like a sketch Proposed

The tabs become Home and Drawing, the view becomes the sheet, the timeline becomes sheet tabs. Finish Drawing goes back to the model.

B · A Model / Drawing switch

A switch that is always in the top bar, each side with its own tabs. More room for tools, but it is the workspace switcher again.

C · Its own window

Sheets open in a second window or browser tab, beside the model. Good on two screens; two shells to keep in step, and two things to save.
