- 2026-11-03 · **A thread's lead-in runs through a chamfered end**: a thread that reaches a chamfered shaft end or a countersunk hole mouth now carries its lead-in cone on through the chamfer, so no collar of the chamfer is left standing; past a chamfer down to a stem or a countersink into a counterbore it stops at the stem's or the counterbore's radius (ADR-0056's sixth amendment).
