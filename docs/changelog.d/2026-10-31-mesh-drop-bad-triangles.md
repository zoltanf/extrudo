- 2026-10-31 · **Flat and duplicate triangles no longer stop a mesh import**: triangles that repeat a corner or an earlier triangle (either winding) are dropped before the closed-solid check, with a warning naming the counts ("4 flat and 1 duplicate triangles were dropped"); a mesh that is still open words them into its refusal. Exporters produce such triangles and slicers ignore them; `mms-m5-nut.stl`-style files import now (ADR-0066's 2026-10-31 amendment).
