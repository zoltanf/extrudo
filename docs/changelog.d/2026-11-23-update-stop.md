- 2026-11-23 · **Stop during a long update**: when one feature of an update takes longer than 30 s, the notice offers Stop next to Cancel; Stop keeps your change and updates the model without that feature. While a Cancel (or Ctrl+Z, which now does the same during a long update) waits for the running feature, the notice says "Cancelling…" and Stop ends the wait. Stop is never highlighted.
