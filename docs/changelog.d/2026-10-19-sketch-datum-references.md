- 2026-10-19 · **Dimensions and constraints to the origin and axes** (ADR-0093, #61): the Sketch Dimension tool and the constraint tools pick the origin point and the X, Y and Z axes, so a line can be dimensioned to the Y axis or a point made coincident with the origin; the picked datum comes in as fixed construction geometry that follows it, in sketches on faces and offset planes too.
